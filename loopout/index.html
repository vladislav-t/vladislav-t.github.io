Frequently asked questions

A line won't move. Something is blocking its way. Lines slide in the direction of their arrow, so clear the blocking line first. Use a Hint to light up a line you can tap right now.

I lost a heart. You lose a heart when you tap a blocked line. Watch an optional video or restart the level to continue.

My purchase is missing. Open Settings → Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to the Apple ID or Google account you used to buy.

How do I remove ads? Buy “No Ads” in the Shop. Optional reward videos stay available if you want extra hints.

Does my progress sync between devices? Progress is saved on your device. Game Center (iOS) keeps your achievements and leaderboard scores.

How do I change ad privacy choices? Open Settings → Privacy & consent in the app.
