Privacy PolicyLOOPOUT: Arrow Art Puzzle

Support

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game LOOPOUT: Arrow Art Puzzle (“the app”, “we”) handles on iOS and Android. The app has no user accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, coins, hints, pixel pictures, daily streak, settings and purchases you made) is saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data. We cannot see or recover it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show, measure and limit ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; Advertising ID on Android);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (views, clicks) and diagnostic data.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy & consent. On iOS you can also deny or revoke tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you buy “No Ads”, ads that interrupt play are no longer shown.

Google's use of data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

We may use Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config to understand how the game is played (for example which levels are too hard), to fix crashes and to tune game settings. This uses an app instance identifier, device and app information, in-game events and crash logs. It does not identify you personally and is not used to show you ads.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment card details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made so that we can unlock it.

Game Center (iOS)

If you are signed in to Game Center, your achievements and leaderboard scores are sent to Apple under your Game Center profile. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above, for the purposes described here.

Data retention and your rights

Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's retention policies. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data and to object to its processing. Because we do not hold personal data on our servers, the fastest way is usually to reset your advertising identifier, change your consent in the app, or contact Google. You can also contact us and we will help.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
